# Filming guide (one page)

How you film decides how well the app works. These are the same rules that commercial basketball-video services require.

## Must do
- **Use a tripod.** Don't hand-hold the camera. A moving camera breaks tracking and the court map.
- **Raise the camera** about 8–15 ft (2.5–4.5 m), e.g. top of the bleachers or a balcony. Shooting from up high keeps players from hiding the ball and the rim.
- **Fit the whole half court in the frame:** baseline, both sidelines, and the 3-point line. For full games, put the camera at half court, high, with both baskets in view.
- **Keep the rim in view the whole time.** The app calls makes and misses by watching the ball pass the rim, so if the rim leaves the frame, that shot is lost.
- **Don't zoom or pan.** Set the framing once before tip-off and leave it. If you must move the camera, stop recording and start a new clip.
- **Film at 1080p, 30 or 60 fps.** 60 fps helps with fast shots. 4K adds nothing; the app scales it down anyway.
- **Use good light.** Turn on all the gym lights and avoid filming into windows or bright doors.

## Nice to have
- **Show the jersey numbers** clearly. Numbers are the only way the app tells players apart; it never uses faces.
- **Keep clips short:** under 10 minutes each (that's the upload limit). Split long games into quarters.
- **HDR is fine.** iPhone HDR/Dolby Vision is converted automatically.
- **Calibrate once per camera spot.** After uploading, click 4 or more court landmarks: lane corners, baseline corners, free-throw line ends. That turns pixels into feet for the shot chart. Five points let the app check its own accuracy.

## Avoid
- Filming from behind the basket at floor level: the backboard hides the shot.
- Hand-holding the camera, zooming, or following the ball. If that's your only option, expect rough results. The heatmap will be "image-space" only.
- Screen recordings of TV broadcasts or downloads from YouTube. Use footage you have the rights to.
- Filming kids without their parents' OK, especially if you'll use cloud AI providers. Local mode keeps everything on the Mac.

## Quick checklist before you press record
- [ ] Tripod, raised, not moving
- [ ] Half court plus rim in frame, with some margin
- [ ] Zoom set and left alone
- [ ] 1080p, 30/60 fps, lights on
